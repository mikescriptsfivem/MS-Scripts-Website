MikeScripts / Local media tool

Add your aircraft & gameplay media.

Choose a product, add your images or clips, and export a media update for the website. Your files stay in this browser. This tool does not publish to GitHub, modify Tebex or require a login.

No files are uploaded. Keep this tab open until you export.

The update ZIP contains only your media and the media configuration. Merge it into the supplied V2 source, then deploy. Files must be under 100 MB each and 200 MB total; smaller compressed clips work better. Existing media referenced in a saved configuration must remain in your website. Reloading this page discards unexported local edits.
