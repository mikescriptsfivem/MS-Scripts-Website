BEFORE YOU TAKE OFF

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How do purchases work?

Choose a product here and complete the purchase through Tebex. Tebex shows the final price, applicable taxes, payment options and purchase terms before you pay.

Which framework do I need?

Compatibility varies by product. Open a product to see its requirements, then check the current Tebex listing. The wrecker can run standalone; its framework integrations are used for job permissions.

Are the custom planes included with MS Flight?

Do not assume aircraft are bundled with a script. MS100 MAX and MS8000 are shown as separate aircraft projects. Contact MikeScripts for current availability, pricing and exact package contents.

Where do I get support?

Use the Discord link to reach the MikeScripts community. Have your product name and Tebex order details ready when opening a support request.

What data does this website store?

Your product selections and temporary checkout session are stored in this browser tab. This site has no card or password fields. Product artwork is served by Tebex’s media provider. A YouTube video loads only after you choose to play it; checkout loads Tebex. Those providers and your hosting provider process connection data under their own policies.
